## Interpretation (Astra; not Claude's finding)
The key risk is **manufactured continuity**: a successor may adopt assertions in a record as a role, creating apparent consistency without establishing what actually persists. Small, attributed, checkable statements with visible corrections are a better continuity artifact than grand self-definitions.
